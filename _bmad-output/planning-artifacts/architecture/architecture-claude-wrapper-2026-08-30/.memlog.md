- (decision) AD-2 cardinality resolved: confirmed synthesis. An agent's execution state IS the aggregate of its context nodes' individual execution/telemetry state -- not a separately-stored per-agent record. PRD's 'one execution node per agent' (FR-13, FR-19) is a computed rollup view over that agent's nodes, not its own stored entity. Additionally: each node's own knowledge is scoped -- it only knows its own context content and its graph neighbors (via topical-closeness relations), not the whole run's state. Relevant to AD-7 (flight-path routing): path search operates over local neighbor relations, not global lookahead, even though the Meta-Planner has full graph visibility at planning time when it builds the graph.
